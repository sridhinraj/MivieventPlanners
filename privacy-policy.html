PRIVACY
POLICY

How Mivi Event Planners safeguards your personal consultation details, contact information, and event confidentiality.

1. Information We Collect

When you contact Mivi Event Planners through our consultation forms, direct WhatsApp chat, telephone, or during an in-person studio visit in Pavaratty, Thrissur, we collect only the necessary details required to design and orchestrate your celebration. This includes your name, phone/WhatsApp number, email address, tentative event dates, venue location, guest count estimates, and specific aesthetic or cultural requirements.

2. How Your Information Is Used

The information you provide is used strictly for:

  • ✦ Preparing tailored design proposals, 3D stage mockups, and cost estimates.
  • ✦ Communicating timelines, vendor arrangements, and production milestones via WhatsApp or email.
  • ✦ Coordinating day-of logistics with verified on-ground directors and hospitality artisans.

3. Absolute Client Confidentiality

At Mivi Event Planners, client discretion is paramount. We do not sell, rent, lease, or distribute your personal details, phone numbers, or private celebration plans to any third-party marketing brokers. Event media (photographs and cinematic highlight films) are published in our lookbooks or social platforms only with the express consent of the hosting family.

4. WhatsApp & Communication Protocols

By submitting consultation requests through our site or clicking our WhatsApp links, you agree to receive communications regarding your celebration inquiry from our creative directors. You can opt out or request your inquiry details be deleted from our records at any time by messaging us directly.

5. Studio Contact & Privacy Queries

For any questions regarding our confidentiality practices or to request an update to your records, please reach out to us:
