FREQUENTLY ASKED
QUESTIONS

Everything you need to know about planning your luxury wedding or bespoke celebration with Mivi Event Planners.

How far in advance should we book Mivi Event Planners? +

For prime wedding dates (peak Kerala wedding seasons in December, January, and auspicious muhurtham days), we recommend securing your date 4 to 8 months in advance. Because we maintain a boutique workload to give every couple dedicated attention, our calendar fills up quickly. For corporate events, stage lighting, or intimate soirées, we can often accommodate bookings on 2 to 4 weeks' notice depending on team schedule.

How does pricing and budgeting work? +

Every celebration we produce is tailored strictly around your personal taste, guest count, and venue scale. After an initial discovery consultation, we prepare a transparent, itemized proposal detailing structural fabrication, botanical styling, mood illumination, and coordination fees. There are no hidden surcharges.

Do you manage destination weddings outside Thrissur? +

Absolutely. While our creative studio is located in Pavaratty, Thrissur, we routinely manage destination celebrations across Kerala, including Kochi waterfront resorts, Guruvayoor sacred temples, Palakkad heritage estates, and Kovalam cliffside villas. We also handle logistics and technical setups for weddings across South India.

How do NRI couples coordinate their wedding remotely? +

Over 60% of our couples are NRIs living in the UAE, Qatar, Oman, UK, and USA. We have built an effortless remote workflow using scheduled video conferences, 3D digital stage maquettes, digital fabric swatches, and timeline milestone trackers. We coordinate directly with family members in Kerala and vetted vendors, so when you land in Kerala, everything is already organized.

Can we visit your design studio to see physical samples? +

Yes, we warmly welcome clients to our studio located on the 1st Floor, Ezra Building, 3Star Nagar, Pavaratty, Thrissur. You can review physical fabric textiles, floral compositions, 3D stage maquettes, and print lookbooks in person. Simply message us on WhatsApp at +91 88772 29985 to schedule your private design session.
